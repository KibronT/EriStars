Who we are

A club built on community

Founded in 2011, EriStars Sports Club brings together young athletes and families across the DMV through soccer, education, and community involvement. Our programs support player development while encouraging teamwork, sportsmanship, and personal growth.

Started by Eritrean-American parents, EriStars is an independent community organization open to every young person who wants to play, learn and belong.
