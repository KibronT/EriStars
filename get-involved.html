Volunteer

Give your time

Volunteers make every EriStars season possible. Parents, alumni and community members can help in many ways.

  • Assisting coaches at training and on match days
  • Helping organize club and community events
  • Logistics, equipment and field-day support
  • Mentoring and academic encouragement

Volunteers working directly with youth may need to complete screening required by the club or league.

Invest in local youth

Sponsor support helps cover the everyday costs of youth soccer, so more young people across the DMV can play, learn and grow.

We welcome conversations with local businesses and organizations that share our values. Contact the club to discuss how your support could be used and how you would like to be recognized.

EriStars is a nonprofit organization (EIN 81-0712069). Please consult your tax advisor about the deductibility of any contribution.
