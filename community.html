Education & community

At EriStars, development extends beyond soccer. We encourage young people to grow as students, teammates, leaders, and active members of their communities.

Student-athletes

Growing students, leaders & citizens

EriStars promotes academics, encourages volunteering and helps young people prepare for life after high school, while staying connected to their heritage and community.

Opportunities vary from season to season. Contact the club for what is currently available.

Academic development

Academics first

We encourage every player to give their best effort in the classroom, and we celebrate academic milestones, including our graduates, as proudly as wins on the field.

  • Encouragement of strong study habits and academic effort
  • Recognition of student achievement and graduation
  • A community of families and mentors who value education
College & career

Path to College

Through its Path to College focus, the club shares information on preparing for higher education, along with scholarship and internship opportunities as they become available.

  • Guidance on preparing for college
  • Scholarship opportunities shared with families
  • Internship opportunities for older players
SSL

Student Service Learning

High school players can take part in volunteer activities that may count toward Student Service Learning (SSL) hours, subject to the requirements of their school system.

  • Volunteer roles supporting club activities and events
  • Check eligibility and documentation with your school before starting
  • Ask the club about current SSL opportunities
Culture & community

Cultural connection

EriStars was founded within the Eritrean-American community. We take pride in that heritage and in building relationships with families from across the DMV.

  • Celebrating Eritrean heritage and identity
  • Bringing families together around the game
  • Welcoming players from every background
Service

Community service

Volunteering is part of club life. Players, parents and supporters help make the season possible and give back to the wider community.

  • Club event and match-day support
  • Community initiatives and outreach
  • Opportunities for parents and alumni to help
