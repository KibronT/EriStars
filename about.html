Our story

Built by parents. Grown by community.

EriStars Sports Club was founded in 2011 by Eritrean-American parents who wanted their children to have a place to play, compete and grow together.

Today the club offers children and youth across the Washington, D.C. metropolitan area a year-round opportunity to enjoy soccer — in an environment that values teamwork and sportsmanship, promotes academics and encourages volunteering.

EriStars operates as the Eritrean-American Youth Sports Association of Maryland, Inc., an independent, non-political and non-religious community organization. Its purpose has always reached beyond the scoreboard: developing athletic ability while encouraging academic achievement, cultural awareness and service to others.

Mission & values

Success through sportsmanship

Our mission is to develop young people through soccer — building skill, discipline and teamwork while encouraging education, cultural pride and community involvement.

Player development

Age-appropriate coaching that builds technique, game understanding and confidence over time.

Teamwork

Players learn to trust, support and compete for one another — on and off the field.

Sportsmanship

We value respect for opponents, officials and teammates over winning at any cost.

Education

Academic effort matters. We encourage players to plan for college and future careers.

Cultural connection

We celebrate Eritrean heritage while welcoming families from across the DMV.

Community involvement

Volunteering and service help young people become active, caring members of their communities.

Our approach

Developing the whole person

EriStars balances athletic development with character and education. The goal is not only better players, but confident young people prepared for what comes next.

  • Development before results. Training focuses on long-term growth, enjoyment and good habits.
  • The student-athlete. Academic encouragement, a Path to College and scholarship information sit alongside soccer.
  • Family and community. Parents, volunteers and coaches work together to support every player.
